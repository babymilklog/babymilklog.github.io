隱私權政策

喝餒餒記錄（BabyMilkLog）・生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

重點

  • 你的紀錄存在你的 iPhone 和你自己的 iCloud。家人分享給你的紀錄簿，存在對方的 iCloud。我們沒有伺服器，看不到 App 裡的任何紀錄和寶寶資料。Apple 提供給我們的技術資料，見第 4 點和第 8 點。
  • 不用註冊帳號。沒有廣告、沒有分析工具、不追蹤你，也沒有使用第三方 SDK。
  • 在 App 裡看得到你的紀錄的，只有登入你這個 Apple 帳號的裝置，和共享同一本紀錄簿的家人。

1. 我們是誰

「喝餒餒記錄」由個人開發者開發。本政策裡的「我們」就是指開發者。有任何問題，請寫信到 babymilklog@icloud.com。

2. App 處理哪些資料

App 處理的是你和家人在 App 裡輸入的內容（或從你選的 CSV 檔匯入的同樣這些內容，見第 7 點），以及使用家人共享時 Apple 提供的成員資料：

  • 寶寶：名字、生日（選填）。
  • 餵奶紀錄：時間、方式（配方奶、瓶餵母乳、左側或右側親餵）、奶量、分鐘數、備註。
  • 記錄者：你在 App 裡填的稱呼（例如「媽媽」）。它會記在你新增的每一筆紀錄上，讓家人知道是誰記的。
  • 家人共享的成員：Apple 的 iCloud 共享提供的成員 iCloud 名字，沒有名字時是 email 或電話號碼。成員清單只顯示在 App 的「設定 → 家人共享」，App 不會把它存起來。加入家人的紀錄簿時，App 會把擁有者的 iCloud 名字存在這支手機上，當作這本紀錄簿的名稱，例如「○○的紀錄」（見第 6 點）。
  • 這支手機的偏好設定：單位、快速奶量、提醒開關、提前幾分鐘提醒、上一次匯入的 CSV 檔名（用於「復原上次匯入」）等。這些只存在這支手機，不會同步到其他手機（iPhone 的備份裡也會有，見第 3 點）。

App 不會讀取你的通訊錄、照片、位置或「健康」App 的資料，也不會要你提供自己的 email 或電話。邀請家人時，是 iPhone 內建的 iCloud 共享畫面讓你選要傳給誰（見第 6 點）。

3. 資料存在哪裡

  • 你的 iPhone：所有紀錄都存在這支手機上的 App 資料庫。
  • 你自己的 iCloud：iPhone 有登入 iCloud 時，App 會用 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit）把紀錄同步到你的 iCloud 帳號。登入同一個 Apple 帳號的其他 iPhone 會看到同一份紀錄。iCloud 由 Apple 提供，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。
  • 沒有登入 iCloud 時：紀錄只存在這支手機（和它的備份）。之後登入 iCloud 時，App 會把這支手機上的紀錄同步到登入的帳號。如果這支手機之前登入的是另一個帳號，App 會先問你（見第 10 點）。
  • 你邀請的家人：他們的手機上也會有一份共享的紀錄（見第 6 點）。
  • 家人分享給你的紀錄簿：存在對方的 iCloud。你在裡面新增的紀錄也存在那裡（見第 6 點）。
  • iPhone 的備份：如果你開啟了 iCloud 備份，或用電腦備份 iPhone，這些備份也會包含 App 裡的紀錄和設定。刪除 App 不會刪除舊的備份。

4. 我們能看到什麼

看不到 App 裡的任何紀錄和寶寶資料。我們沒有伺服器，App 不會把你的紀錄傳給我們或任何第三方。存在 iCloud 的資料由 Apple 依它的 iCloud 安全措施加密保存（見第 11 點），開發者讀不到內容。在 App 裡看得到這些資料的，只有登入同一個 Apple 帳號的裝置，以及共享同一本紀錄簿的家人。

  • 不需要帳號，也不用登入（只用 iPhone 本身的 iCloud）。
  • 沒有廣告。
  • App 裡沒有分析或統計工具，不記錄你在 App 裡的操作，也不跨 App 或網站追蹤你。
  • 沒有使用任何第三方 SDK。

App 只會連到 Apple 的服務：iCloud（同步與家人共享）和 Apple 推播通知服務（讓 iCloud 在背景同步，以及家人更新紀錄時通知你）。在 App 的「設定 → 關於」點「隱私權政策」或「使用說明與聯絡」時，會用 Safari 等瀏覽器開啟這個網站，詳見本頁最下方的說明。

除了 App Store 的下載數量這類整體統計，我們只會從 Apple 收到下面兩種和 App 使用有關的技術資料，都不含紀錄的內容，也不含寶寶的名字：

  • iCloud 的存取紀錄：Apple 給開發者的 iCloud 管理工具，會列出 App 每一次存取 iCloud 的時間、裝置平台、系統版本、資料庫種類和操作種類，以及每個帳號在這個 App 專用的匿名代號，也有整體的用量統計。Apple 說明這些紀錄不會顯示能辨識個人的資料。我們只用來查同步的問題。
  • 當機報告、效能資料和使用統計：只有你打開「與 App 開發者分享」時才有，見第 8 點。

如果你寫信給我們，我們會收到你的 email 地址和信件內容，只用來回覆你。信件存在我們的電子郵件信箱（由 Apple 的 iCloud 郵件提供），問題處理完、不再需要時就會刪除。你也可以隨時寫信要求我們刪除你的來信。來信時不需要提供寶寶的名字或紀錄。

5. 下一餐估計與通知

  • 「下一餐估計」、「接下來幾餐」、「估計準確度」和統計圖表都在你的手機上計算，不會上傳。估計是依過去的紀錄算出的，僅供參考，不能取代醫療建議。對寶寶的喝奶量或健康有疑慮時，請諮詢醫師或護理師。
  • 下一餐提醒（「寶寶的下一餐估計快到了」）是排在手機上的本機通知。你允許通知後才會出現，可以在 App 的「設定 → 提醒」或 iPhone 的「設定 → 通知」關閉。
  • 家人新增、修改或刪除紀錄時（你登入同一個 Apple 帳號的其他裝置有變更時也可能會收到），iCloud 會透過 Apple 的推播服務通知你的手機。推播本身只有「家人更新了餵奶紀錄」這類一般文字。App 會在你的手機上讀取 iCloud 裡的最新紀錄，再把通知改成具體內容（例如「媽媽在 21:05 記錄了配方奶 120 ml」）。這類通知不響、不亮螢幕。
  • 通知可能出現在鎖定畫面。家人更新的通知可能包含記錄者的稱呼、時間和奶量。下一餐提醒會有估計的時間區間。紀錄簿裡有好幾個寶寶時，兩種通知都會有寶寶的名字。鎖定畫面要不要顯示內容，依 iPhone 的「設定 → 通知 → 顯示預覽」。也可以在「設定 → 通知 → 喝餒餒記錄」調整。

6. 和家人共享

  • 只有你邀請的人能加入。邀請連結由 iPhone 內建的 iCloud 共享畫面產生，透過你選的方式（訊息、郵件或拷貝連結）傳送。你在這個畫面選的 email 或電話號碼，由 Apple 用來傳送邀請，並記在這個共享裡。
  • 邀請裡會有寶寶的名字（邀請時選的寶寶）。共享的標題「○○的喝奶記錄」存在 iCloud 的共享裡，○○ 是第一次邀請時選的寶寶。之後寶寶改名或刪除，標題都不會跟著改。停止共享時，標題會和共享一起刪除。
  • 加入的家人看得到這本紀錄裡所有的寶寶（名字、生日）和所有紀錄（包括備註和記錄者），也可以新增、修改和刪除寶寶和紀錄。
  • 成員清單：共享裡的每個人（包括你）都看得到其他成員的 iCloud 名字。沒有名字時，會顯示 email 或電話號碼。已經邀請、還沒加入的人也可能列在清單上。這些資料由 Apple 的 iCloud 共享提供。
  • 在家人的手機上，加入的紀錄簿會用擁有者的 iCloud 名字命名，例如「○○的紀錄」。
  • 共享的紀錄存在邀請人的 iCloud，使用邀請人的 iCloud 儲存空間。家人新增的紀錄也存在那裡。
  • 停止共享：邀請人到「設定 → 家人共享」按「停止共享」，所有家人都會被移出。
  • 移除某一位家人：邀請人到「設定 → 家人共享」點「管理家人」，在 iCloud 共享畫面移除那位成員。
  • 離開共享：家人到「設定 → 家人共享」按「離開共享」。
  • 自己離開時，這本紀錄會馬上從那支手機移除。被移出或停止共享時，會在他的手機下次和 iCloud 同步時移除。他之前自己匯出的檔案，則不在 App 能控制的範圍。
  • 你在家人的紀錄簿裡新增的紀錄（含你的稱呼與備註）存在對方的 iCloud。離開共享後，這些紀錄仍會留在那本紀錄簿，對方和其他家人還看得到。想移除，請在離開前自己刪除，或請擁有者刪除（刪除後會留下看不到的「已刪除」標記，見第 10 點）。

7. 匯出與匯入

  • 只有你按下「匯出」時，App 才會產生 CSV 或 JSON 檔，並開啟 iPhone 的分享畫面，由你決定存到哪裡或傳給誰（例如「檔案」App、AirDrop 或郵件）。檔案離開 App 之後由你保管。分享前請留意：檔案裡有寶寶的名字和紀錄，JSON 檔還包含寶寶的生日和刪除過的紀錄。
  • CSV 檔不含已刪除的紀錄。JSON 檔包含完整資料，也包含這本紀錄簿裡刪除過的紀錄和寶寶（含原本的內容，見第 10 點）。
  • 匯入 CSV：只有你選擇匯入時，App 才會在你的手機上讀取你在「檔案」裡選的那一個檔案。匯入的紀錄會加進目前開啟的紀錄簿，和你自己記的一樣同步，紀錄簿有共享時家人也看得到。匯入到家人分享給你的紀錄簿時，紀錄會存到對方的 iCloud。檔案裡的寶寶名字、備註和記錄者也會一起匯入（可以選擇記到現有的寶寶，或用檔案裡的名字新增寶寶）。檔案不會傳給我們。

8. Apple 提供的當機報告、效能資料與使用統計

如果你在 iPhone 的「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 分析與改進」打開了「與 App 開發者分享」，Apple 可能會把這個 App 的當機報告、效能資料和使用統計（例如裝置型號、iOS 版本、啟動時間、App 開啟的次數）分享給我們。這些資料由 Apple 收集和處理。我們看到的是 Apple 整理好的報告和統計數字，不含你在 App 裡輸入的紀錄內容。我們只用來修正錯誤、改進 App。你隨時可以在同一個地方關閉。

9. 兒童

這個 App 是給爸媽和照顧者用的，不是設計給兒童使用。寶寶的名字、生日和餵奶紀錄由爸媽或照顧者輸入，只存在第 3 點說的地方，也可能出現在通知（見第 5 點）、你傳出的邀請和你匯出的檔案裡。App 不會把任何人（包括兒童）的資料傳給我們。

10. 保留與刪除

你的資料會一直保留，直到你刪除。我們手上沒有你在 App 裡的紀錄，所以沒辦法替你刪除。下面的方法都可以自己完成。

  • 刪除一筆紀錄或一個寶寶：在 App 裡刪除後，你和家人的手機上都不會再顯示。為了讓每支手機都知道哪些被刪除，App 會在這本紀錄簿裡留下畫面上看不到的「已刪除」標記，目前會保留原本的內容（例如寶寶的名字、備註和記錄者）。這些標記和紀錄簿一起存在紀錄簿擁有者的 iCloud，以及每支看得到這本紀錄簿的手機上，JSON 匯出檔也會包含（見第 7 點）。紀錄簿的擁有者刪除 iCloud 上這個 App 的資料時（見下方），標記會一起清除，各手機上的 App 下次同步時也會清除。沒有使用 iCloud 時，標記只存在這支手機，刪除 App 才會清除。已經匯出的檔案和 iPhone 的備份不會因此清除。
  • 刪除這支手機上的資料：刪除 App。iCloud 上的資料不會因此刪除。用同一個 iCloud 帳號重新安裝時，紀錄會自動下載回來。
  • 登出或更換 iCloud 帳號：登出時，你自己的紀錄會留在這支手機上。家人分享給你的紀錄簿會從這支手機移除，家人那邊的紀錄不受影響。如果共享還在，用原本的帳號登入後可以再找回。換成另一個 iCloud 帳號時，如果這支手機上有紀錄，App 會先問你要上傳到新的帳號，還是從這支手機移除。已經在原本帳號 iCloud 裡的紀錄不會因此刪除。
  • iPhone 的備份：如果你開啟了 iCloud 備份或用電腦備份 iPhone，這些備份也包含 App 裡的紀錄和設定。刪除 App 不會刪除舊的備份。要清除，請在 iPhone 的 iCloud 備份設定或電腦上刪除那份備份。
  • 刪除 iCloud 上的資料：在 iPhone 的「設定」點你的名字 →「iCloud」→「管理帳號儲存空間」→「喝餒餒記錄」→ 刪除資料（選項名稱可能因 iOS 版本略有不同）。這會刪除你 iCloud 裡這個 App 的所有紀錄，你分享給家人的紀錄（包括家人在裡面記的）也會一起消失。App 下次同步時也會清除這支手機上的紀錄。
  • 家人分享給你的紀錄：存在對方的 iCloud。你可以離開共享（見第 6 點），但不能刪除對方的資料。離開後，你在那本紀錄簿裡新增的紀錄（含你的稱呼與備註）仍會留在對方那裡。想移除，請在離開前自己刪除，或請擁有者刪除。刪除後，對方的 iCloud 仍會留下保留原本內容的「已刪除」標記，只有擁有者刪除自己 iCloud 上這個 App 的資料時才會清除。

11. 安全

  • 手機上的資料受 iOS 的資料保護機制保護。
  • 同步經由 Apple 的 iCloud，傳輸過程有加密，並依 Apple 的 iCloud 安全措施加密保存。App 本身沒有再另外加密這些資料。
  • 我們沒有存放你資料的伺服器。
  • 建議替 iPhone 設定密碼、保護好你的 Apple 帳號，並且只邀請你信任的人。

如果 App 處理資料的方式改變，我們會更新這一頁，並修改上方的生效日期。重大的改變也會寫在 App Store 的更新說明裡。

Privacy Policy

BabyMilkLog (喝餒餒記錄) · Effective date: November 20, 2026

In short

  • Your records stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. A log that family shares with you is stored in their iCloud. We have no servers and cannot see any records or baby details in the app. For the technical data Apple provides to us, see sections 4 and 8.
  • No account to create. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • In the app, your records can only be seen on devices signed in to your Apple Account and by the family members who share the same log with you.

1. Who we are

BabyMilkLog is made by an individual developer. “We” in this policy means the developer. If you have any questions, email babymilklog@icloud.com.

2. What the app handles

The app handles what you and your family type into it (or the same kinds of data imported from a CSV file you choose, see section 7), plus member details that Apple provides when you share with family:

  • Baby: name, and birthday (optional).
  • Feeds: time, type (formula, bottled breast milk, left or right breastfeeding), amount, minutes, and a note.
  • Who logged it: the name you choose in the app (for example “Mom”). It is saved on each record you add, so your family knows who logged it.
  • Family sharing members: the members’ iCloud names provided by Apple’s iCloud sharing, or their email address or phone number when no name is available. The member list is only shown in the app’s Settings → 家人共享 (Share with Family), and the app does not store it. When you join a family member’s log, the app saves the owner’s iCloud name on this iPhone as the name of the log, for example “○○的紀錄” (“○○’s log”) (see section 6).
  • Preferences on this iPhone: unit, quick amounts, reminder on/off, how many minutes early to remind you, the name of the last imported CSV file (for 復原上次匯入 / Undo last import), and similar settings. These stay on this iPhone and are not synced to other iPhones. They are included in iPhone backups (see section 3).

The app does not read your contacts, photos, location or Health data, and never asks for your own email address or phone number. When you invite family, the iPhone’s built-in iCloud sharing screen lets you choose who to send it to (see section 6).

3. Where your data is stored

  • Your iPhone: all records are stored in the app’s database on your iPhone.
  • Your own iCloud: when your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, the app uses Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) to sync records to your iCloud account. Other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account see the same records. iCloud is provided by Apple and is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Not signed in to iCloud: records stay only on this iPhone (and its backups). If you sign in to iCloud later, the app syncs the records on this iPhone to that account. If this iPhone was signed in to a different account before, the app asks you first (see section 10).
  • Family you invite: their iPhones also keep a copy of the shared records (see section 6).
  • A log family shares with you: it is stored in their iCloud. Records you add to it are stored there too (see section 6).
  • iPhone backups: if you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, those backups include the app’s records and settings. Deleting the app does not delete old backups.

4. What we can see

None of the records or baby details in the app. We have no servers, and the app never sends your records to us or to any third party. Data in iCloud is stored encrypted by Apple under its iCloud security measures (see section 11), and the developer cannot read its content. In the app, this data can only be seen on devices signed in to the same Apple Account and by the family who share the same log.

  • No account and no sign-in (the app only uses your iPhone’s iCloud).
  • No ads.
  • The app has no analytics. It doesn’t record what you do in the app, and we don’t track you across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs.

The app only connects to Apple services: iCloud (sync and family sharing) and the Apple Push Notification service (so iCloud can sync in the background, and to tell you when family updates the log). When you tap 隱私權政策 (Privacy Policy) or 使用說明與聯絡 (Help and Contact) in the app’s Settings → 關於 (About), this website opens in Safari or another web browser. See the note at the bottom of this page.

Apart from overall App Store statistics such as download counts, we only receive two kinds of technical data from Apple about how the app is used. Neither includes the content of your records or your baby’s name:

  • iCloud access logs: Apple’s iCloud tools for developers list each time the app accesses iCloud, with the time, device platform, OS version, the type of database and operation, and an anonymous ID for each account that is specific to this app. They also show overall usage statistics. Apple states that these logs do not show personally identifiable information. We use them only to look into sync problems.
  • Crash reports, performance data and usage statistics: only if you turn on “Share With App Developers” (see section 8).

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply. Emails are kept in our mailbox (provided by Apple’s iCloud Mail) and deleted once the question is resolved and they are no longer needed. You can also ask us at any time to delete your emails. You don’t need to send your baby’s name or records.

5. Next-feed estimate and notifications

  • The next-feed estimate, the upcoming-feeds list, the estimate-accuracy card and the charts are calculated on your iPhone and are never uploaded. The estimate is based on your past records. It is for reference only and cannot replace medical advice. If you have concerns about how much your baby drinks or about their health, ask a doctor or nurse.
  • The next-feed reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. It only appears if you allow notifications, and you can turn it off in the app (Settings → 提醒 / Reminders) or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.
  • When family adds, edits or deletes a record (and possibly when another device signed in to your Apple Account makes a change), iCloud notifies your iPhone through Apple’s push service. The push itself only carries generic text like “family updated the feeding log”. The app then reads the latest records from iCloud on your iPhone and rewrites the notification with details (for example, “Mom logged formula 120 ml at 21:05”). These notifications make no sound and don’t light up the screen.
  • Notifications can appear on the lock screen. A family update may include the caregiver’s name, the time and the amount. A next-feed reminder includes the estimated time window. When a log has more than one baby, both kinds include the baby’s name. Whether the lock screen shows this depends on iPhone Settings → Notifications → Show Previews. You can also change it in Settings → Notifications → 喝餒餒記錄.

6. Sharing with family

  • Only people you invite can join. The invitation link is created by the iPhone’s built-in iCloud sharing screen and sent the way you choose (Messages, Mail or Copy Link). Apple uses the email addresses or phone numbers you pick on that screen to send the invitation, and keeps them in the share.
  • The invitation includes your baby’s name (the baby selected when you invite). The share’s title “○○的喝奶記錄” (“○○’s feeding log”) is stored in the share in iCloud, where ○○ is the baby selected when you first invite someone. It does not change if the baby is later renamed or deleted. When you stop sharing, the title is deleted along with the share.
  • Family who join can see every baby in the shared log (name, birthday) and every record (including notes and who logged it). They can also add, edit and delete babies and records.
  • Member list: everyone in the share, including you, can see the other members’ iCloud names, or their email address or phone number when no name is available. People who were invited but have not joined yet may also be listed. This comes from Apple’s iCloud sharing.
  • On a family member’s iPhone, a joined log is named after the owner’s iCloud name, for example “○○的紀錄” (“○○’s log”).
  • Shared records are stored in the inviter’s iCloud and use the inviter’s iCloud storage. Records added by family are stored there too.
  • Stop sharing: the inviter goes to Settings → 家人共享 (Share with Family) and taps 停止共享 (Stop Sharing). Everyone else is removed.
  • Remove one person: the inviter goes to Settings → 家人共享 (Share with Family), taps 管理家人 (Manage Family), and removes that member on the iCloud sharing screen.
  • Leave: a family member goes to Settings → 家人共享 (Share with Family) and taps 離開共享 (Leave Sharing).
  • When someone leaves, the shared log is removed from that iPhone right away. When someone is removed, or sharing is stopped, it is removed from their iPhone the next time it syncs with iCloud. Files they exported earlier are outside the app’s control.
  • Records you add to a family member’s log (including your name and notes) are stored in their iCloud. After you leave, they stay in that log, and the owner and other family can still see them. To remove them, delete them yourself before you leave, or ask the owner to delete them (deleting leaves a hidden “deleted” marker, see section 10).

7. Export and import

  • A CSV or JSON file is created only when you tap Export. The app opens the iPhone share sheet, and you decide where it goes (for example the Files app, AirDrop or Mail). Once a file leaves the app, it is in your hands. It contains your baby’s name and records, and a JSON file also contains your baby’s birthday and deleted records, so share it with care.
  • CSV files leave out deleted records. JSON files contain the full data, including the records and babies deleted from this log, with their original content (see section 10).
  • CSV import: only when you choose to import, the app reads the one file you pick in Files, on your iPhone. Imported records are added to the log that is currently open and sync like the ones you log yourself. If the log is shared, family can see them too. Records imported into a log that family shares with you are stored in their iCloud. Baby names, notes and who logged each record are imported from the file too (you can add the records to an existing baby, or add a new baby with the name from the file). The file is never sent to us.

8. Crash reports, performance data and usage statistics from Apple

If you turned on “Share With App Developers” in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share this app’s crash reports, performance data and usage statistics (such as device model, iOS version, launch time and how often the app is opened) with us. Apple collects and processes this data. We see the reports and statistics that Apple prepares, which do not include the content of the records you enter in the app. We use them only to fix bugs and improve the app. You can turn this off in the same place at any time.

9. Children

This app is for parents and caregivers. It is not designed for children to use. The baby’s name, birthday and feeding records are entered by parents or caregivers and are kept only in the places listed in section 3. They may also appear in notifications (see section 5), in invitations you send and in files you export. The app does not send anyone’s data to us, including children’s.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. We don’t hold your records in the app, so we can’t delete them for you, but you can do all of this yourself:

  • Delete a record or a baby: once deleted in the app, it no longer appears on your iPhone or on your family’s iPhones. So that every iPhone knows what was deleted, the app keeps a hidden “deleted” marker in the log, which currently keeps the original content (for example the baby’s name, notes and who logged the record). These markers are stored with the log in its owner’s iCloud and on every iPhone that can see the log, and JSON exports include them (see section 7). When the log’s owner deletes this app’s data in iCloud (see below), the markers are removed too, and the app on each iPhone clears them the next time it syncs. Without iCloud, the markers are only on this iPhone and are removed only when you delete the app. Files you already exported and iPhone backups are not cleared this way.
  • Delete the data on this iPhone: delete the app. Your iCloud data is not deleted. If you reinstall with the same iCloud account, your records download again.
  • Signing out of or changing your iCloud account: when you sign out, your own records stay on this iPhone. Logs that family shared with you are removed from this iPhone, and the records on your family’s side are not affected. If the share still exists, you can get them back by signing in with the original account. When you sign in with a different iCloud account and this iPhone has records, the app first asks whether to upload them to the new account or remove them from this iPhone. Records already in the original account’s iCloud are not deleted.
  • iPhone backups: if you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, those backups include the app’s records and settings. Deleting the app does not delete old backups. To remove the data, delete that backup in your iPhone’s iCloud Backup settings or on your computer.
  • Delete the data in iCloud: in iPhone Settings, tap your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → 喝餒餒記錄 → delete the data (names may differ slightly between iOS versions). This deletes all of this app’s records in your iCloud, including the log you shared with family and the records family added to it. The app also clears the records on this iPhone the next time it syncs.
  • Logs other family members shared with you: these are stored in their iCloud. You can leave the share (see section 6), but you can’t delete their data. After you leave, the records you added to their log (including your name and notes) stay there. To remove them, delete them yourself before you leave, or ask the owner to delete them. Deleting still leaves a hidden “deleted” marker with the original content in the owner’s iCloud. It is removed only when the owner deletes this app’s data in their own iCloud.

11. Security

  • Data on your iPhone is protected by iOS data protection.
  • Sync goes through Apple’s iCloud. Data is encrypted in transit and stored encrypted under Apple’s iCloud security measures. The app does not add its own extra encryption.
  • We have no servers that store your data.
  • We recommend setting a passcode on your iPhone, keeping your Apple Account secure, and only inviting people you trust.
