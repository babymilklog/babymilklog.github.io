喝餒餒記錄

記錄寶寶每一餐的 iPhone App

用大按鈕快速記下配方奶、瓶餵母乳或左右側親餵，看今天喝了多少、距離上一餐多久，以及每日總量與次數的圖表。App 會依你最近的紀錄估計下一餐大概什麼時候（僅供參考，不能取代醫療建議），快到的時候也可以提醒你。你還可以邀請另一半用他自己的 iCloud 帳號一起記錄，誰記的都會同步。紀錄只存在你的 iPhone 和你自己的 iCloud（家人分享給你的紀錄存在對方的 iCloud），開發者看不到這些紀錄。不用註冊、沒有廣告，也不追蹤你。

BabyMilkLog is an iPhone app for logging your baby’s feeds with big buttons: formula, bottled breast milk, or left/right breastfeeding. It shows how much your baby has had today, the time since the last feed, and charts of daily totals and feed counts. It estimates from your recent records when the next feed may come (for reference only and not a substitute for medical advice), and it can remind you when that time is near. You can also invite a partner to log together with their own iCloud account, and records sync no matter who logs them. Your records stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud (a log that family shares with you is stored in their iCloud). The developer cannot see these records. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
