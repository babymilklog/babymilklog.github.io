常見問題。本頁的「設定」是指 App 下方的「設定」分頁，iPhone 內建的設定 App 會寫成 iPhone 的「設定」。找不到答案的話，請寫信到 babymilklog@icloud.com。

怎麼和家人一起記錄？

需要兩個不同的 Apple 帳號（例如你和另一半各自的 iCloud）。這是 App 裡的「家人共享」，和 iPhone 的「設定」裡 Apple 的「家人共享」（Family Sharing）無關，也不需要加入同一個家人共享群組。

  1. 你：在「設定 → 家人共享」點「邀請家人」，用「訊息」、「郵件」或「拷貝連結」傳給家人。只有受邀的人能加入，所以邀請用的電話或電子郵件要是家人 Apple 帳號使用的那一個。
  2. 家人：從 App Store 安裝「喝餒餒記錄」，確認 iPhone 登入的是受邀的那個 Apple 帳號，再點開你傳的邀請連結。不需要先在 App 裡新增寶寶。
  3. App 會自動開啟並加入。之後你們看到的是同一份紀錄，雙方都能新增和修改，會自動同步。每一筆都會顯示記錄者的「顯示名稱」，請各自在「設定」的「我」填寫。沒填時會顯示「家人」，之後再填也不會改到已經記下的紀錄。

家人點開連結後沒有加入時，請先確認邀請用的電話或電子郵件是家人 Apple 帳號使用的那一個。不是的話，請在「設定 → 家人共享」點「管理家人」，用正確的電話或電子郵件重新邀請。

要結束共享，擁有者可以在「設定 → 家人共享」點「停止共享」，家人可以在同一個畫面點「離開共享」。

同一個 Apple 帳號的多支 iPhone 不需要邀請，登入同一個 iCloud 就會同步。

紀錄沒有同步？

  • 兩支手機都要登入 iCloud，並且連上網路。
  • 在 iPhone 的「設定」裡，確認使用 iCloud 的 App 中「喝餒餒記錄」沒有被關掉。
  • 共享的紀錄使用邀請人的 iCloud 空間。空間滿了就無法同步。
  • 到 App 的「設定 → iCloud」看同步狀態，再點「立即同步」。
  • 關閉通知權限時，家人的新紀錄可能要等你打開 App 才會更新。

下一餐提醒怎麼運作？

App 在你的手機上，依最近幾週的餵奶紀錄（時段、上一餐喝的量、最近的節奏）估計下一餐，在估計區間開始前提醒你。可以在「設定 → 提醒」用「提前提醒」調整提前幾分鐘。紀錄不到約 30 餐時，改用最近 7 天同一時段（白天或夜間）的餵奶間隔。紀錄更少時，先以上一餐後 3 小時估計。「這台手機接收提醒」是每支手機各自設定，關閉只影響那一支手機。提醒需要允許通知。

提醒會以時效性通知送出。想在「睡眠」等專注模式下也收到提醒，請到 iPhone 的「設定 → 通知 → 喝餒餒記錄」確認「時效性通知」已打開，並確認那個專注模式允許時效性通知。

「下一餐估計」準嗎？

它是依你的紀錄算出的估計，僅供參考。「統計」分頁最下面的「估計準確度」會拿你自己的紀錄回頭比較，看過去的估計有幾次落在估計區間內。寶寶每天的狀況都不一樣，請以寶寶的實際反應為準。這個 App 不提供醫療建議，也不能取代醫療建議。對寶寶的喝奶量、體重或健康有疑慮時，請諮詢醫師或護理師。

怎麼匯出紀錄？

在「設定 → 資料」選「匯出 CSV（Excel 可開）」或「匯出 JSON」，再從分享畫面存到「檔案」或傳送出去。會匯出目前開啟的紀錄簿裡所有寶寶的紀錄。CSV 是 Excel 可以開啟的表格檔，使用目前的單位，不含已刪除的紀錄和寶寶的生日，可以用「匯入 CSV」匯回 App。JSON 是一種文字資料格式，內容是完整的資料，也包含刪除過的紀錄，適合自己留存。App 目前不能匯入 JSON 檔。

怎麼匯入 CSV？

在「設定 → 資料」點「匯入 CSV」，再從「檔案」App 挑選檔案。可以匯入這個 App 匯出的 CSV，以及欄位是「寶寶、時間、總量 (毫升)、便條」的檔案。後者沒有餵奶方式，匯入時要選全部記成配方奶或瓶餵母乳。匯入前會先顯示預覽，包括讀到幾筆、會略過幾筆重複的，以及檔案裡每個寶寶名字要記到哪個寶寶。預設記到同名的寶寶，沒有同名的會新增一個，也可以改選。按「匯入 N 筆」才會寫入。想取消，可以在「設定 → 資料」點「復原上次匯入」，下一次匯入之前都可以復原。檔案在你的手機上讀取，不會傳給我們。匯入的紀錄會加進目前開啟的紀錄簿，和平常一樣同步（家人分享給你的紀錄簿會存到對方的 iCloud）。備註會照檔案匯入，這個 App 匯出的 CSV 也會帶入記錄者。

換手機或重新安裝，紀錄還在嗎？

用同一個 iCloud 帳號登入，紀錄會自動下載回來。之前加入的家人紀錄，App 打開時也會自動從 iCloud 找回。找回後如果沒有自動打開，可以在「設定 → 紀錄簿」切換過去。沒有找回來，而且畫面停在第一次設定時，請點「找回之前加入的家人紀錄」。已經在使用 App 的話，請到「設定 → 家人共享」點「找回家人分享的紀錄」。這兩種方式都要用加入時的同一個 iCloud 帳號。之前沒有登入 iCloud 的話，紀錄只存在原本那支手機，刪除 App 後無法從 iCloud 找回。

How do I log together with family?

You need two different Apple Accounts (for example, yours and your partner’s iCloud). This is the app’s own 家人共享 (Share with Family) screen, not Apple Family Sharing in iPhone Settings. You don’t need to be in the same Family Sharing group.

  1. You: go to Settings → 家人共享 (Share with Family), tap 邀請家人 (Invite Family), and send the invitation by Messages, Mail or Copy Link. Only invited people can join, so invite the phone number or email address on your family member’s Apple Account.
  2. Your family member: install 喝餒餒記錄 from the App Store, make sure the iPhone is signed in to the invited Apple Account, then open the link you sent. There is no need to add a baby in the app first.
  3. The app opens and joins automatically. From then on you both see the same log, both can add and edit records, and everything syncs. Each record shows the 顯示名稱 (Display name) of the person who logged it. Each of you can set it under Settings → 我 (Me). If it is empty, records show 家人 (Family). Setting it later does not change records already logged.

If your family member opens the link but does not join, first check that you invited the phone number or email address on their Apple Account. If not, go to Settings → 家人共享 (Share with Family), tap 管理家人 (Manage Family), and invite them again with the right phone number or email address.

To stop sharing, the owner can tap 停止共享 (Stop Sharing) in Settings → 家人共享 (Share with Family). A family member can tap 離開共享 (Leave Sharing) on the same screen.

Several iPhones on the same Apple Account don’t need an invitation. They sync automatically through the same iCloud.

Records are not syncing

  • Both iPhones must be signed in to iCloud and online.
  • In iPhone Settings, make sure 喝餒餒記錄 is not turned off in the list of apps using iCloud.
  • A shared log uses the inviter’s iCloud storage. If it is full, sync stops.
  • In the app, open Settings → iCloud to see the sync status, then tap 立即同步 (Sync Now).
  • If notifications are turned off, family updates may not arrive until you open the app.

How do next-feed reminders work?

The app estimates the next feed on your iPhone from your records of the last few weeks (time of day, how much the last feed was, the recent rhythm) and reminds you before the estimated window starts. You can set how many minutes early with 提前提醒 (Remind early) in Settings → 提醒 (Reminders). With fewer than about 30 feeds, it uses the gaps in the same part of the day (daytime or night) over the last 7 days. With even fewer, it assumes 3 hours after the last feed. The 這台手機接收提醒 (This iPhone receives reminders) switch is set on each iPhone separately, and turning it off only affects that iPhone. Reminders need notification permission.

Reminders are sent as Time Sensitive notifications. To get them during a Focus such as Sleep, go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → 喝餒餒記錄, make sure Time Sensitive Notifications is on, and check that the Focus allows time-sensitive notifications.

How accurate is the next-feed estimate?

It is an estimate based on your records and is for reference only. The 估計準確度 (Estimate accuracy) card at the bottom of the 統計 (Stats) tab looks back at your own records and shows how many past estimates fell inside the estimated window. Every day is different, so follow your baby’s cues. This app does not give medical advice and cannot replace medical advice. If you have concerns about your baby’s feeding, weight or health, ask a doctor or nurse.

How do I export my records?

Go to Settings → 資料 (Data) and choose 匯出 CSV (Export CSV, opens in Excel) or 匯出 JSON (Export JSON), then save it to Files or send it from the share sheet. The export covers every baby in the log that is currently open. CSV is a spreadsheet file that Excel can open. It uses your current unit and leaves out deleted records and birth dates, and you can bring it back into the app with 匯入 CSV (Import CSV). JSON is a plain-text data format that holds the full data, including records you deleted, and is a good way to keep your own copy. The app cannot import JSON files at this time.

How do I import a CSV file?

Go to Settings → 資料 (Data), tap 匯入 CSV (Import CSV), and pick the file in the Files app. You can import a CSV exported by this app, or a file with the columns 寶寶, 時間, 總量 (毫升), 便條 (baby, time, total ml, note). The second kind has no feeding method, so you choose whether all rows are 配方奶 (formula) or 瓶餵母乳 (bottled breast milk). A preview first shows how many records were read, how many duplicates will be skipped and which baby each name in the file goes to. By default a name goes to the baby with the same name, or a new baby is added if none matches, and you can change this. Nothing is saved until you tap 匯入 N 筆 (Import N records). To undo, go to Settings → 資料 (Data) and tap 復原上次匯入 (Undo last import). This works until your next import. The file is read on your iPhone and is never sent to us. Imported records are added to the log that is currently open and sync as usual (a log that family shares with you is stored in their iCloud). Notes are imported from the file, and a CSV exported by this app also brings in who logged each record.

Will my records survive a new iPhone or a reinstall?

Yes, if you sign in with the same iCloud account. Your records download automatically. When the app opens, it also looks in iCloud for logs that family shared with you. If a recovered log does not open by itself, switch to it in Settings → 紀錄簿 (Logs). If nothing comes back and you see the first-time setup screen, tap 找回之前加入的家人紀錄 (Recover family logs you joined). If you are already using the app, go to Settings → 家人共享 (Share with Family) and tap 找回家人分享的紀錄 (Recover shared logs). Both need the same iCloud account you joined with. If you were not signed in to iCloud before, your records were stored only on that iPhone and cannot be recovered from iCloud after the app is deleted.
